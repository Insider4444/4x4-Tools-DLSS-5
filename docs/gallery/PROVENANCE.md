# Comparison image provenance

The publisher supplied six before/after pairs from Downloads/Examples and
authorized their use in the project's comparison gallery. The plain filename
is the original; the matching (1) image is enhanced, except scene 17186 whose
enhanced filename ends in (2). The publisher confirmed this mapping. All six
pairs were visually checked for corresponding subjects and framing.

The original file prefix is
Grand.Theft.Auto.VI.An.Extended.Look.2026.2160p.NF.WEB-DL.DDP5.1.H.265-Friday4KPopcorn_.
Source images are 3840 × 2160 PNGs. Gallery files are 960 × 540 PNG copies,
resized identically with bicubic interpolation. No generative edit or color
adjustment was applied while preparing the gallery. Original files were not modified.

These supplied results predate final Photoshop v1.2 testing. Their generating
plug-in version, runtime and settings were not recorded. They illustrate the
publisher's examples; they do not establish a controlled v1.2 benchmark,
support on another GPU, or an outcome for every image.
